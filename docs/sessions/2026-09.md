**The four defects, all found by verifying my own work rather than by looking for them**

1. **fmv-recalc's historical fallback had failed on 350 of 350 runs** and reported
   `historicalFallback=0` — indistinguishable from "nothing to do" on every instrument anyone reads.
   Found by asking whether the change I had *just* deployed actually executed, which needed the runtime
   logs because `pipeline_runs` cannot see it and pgss cannot see through `query_sql`.
2. **My own fix then failed its exit condition** — 23,800 editions "covered" for ~895 net progress. The
   admission predicate tested `algo_version NOT LIKE '1.7.%'`, a staleness proxy from when 1.7.x was the
   only writer; there are now eight and seven do not match, so this step and the thin-sales guard were
   overwriting each other every tick. **99.6% of admissions were false.**
3. **`/insights/pack-reality` was telling the public "No +EV packs right now"** while `pack_ev_latest`
   held 4,640 fresh rows. The 48 h freshness clause alone emptied the board; the three survivors were
   107–130 h stale behind the dead Top Shot endpoint.
4. **`sales-counterparty-backfill` has walked past Flow's spork wall** and burns ~9.2 h/day for zero —
   because Flow REST answers pruned history with **HTTP 200 + `execution:"Pending"` + zero events**, so
   an unreachable era is indistinguishable from a throttle miss. Filed; the fix needs `wrangler`.
