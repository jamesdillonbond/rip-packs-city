Read-only. Nothing in `sales`, `moments` or `editions` was changed.

## Method
- Flow REST `/v1/scripts` (sealed block), called from the DB with `net.http_post`. The sandbox has no egress to Flow.
- Contract: TopShot at `0x0b2a3299cc857e29`. The deployed source (`/v1/accounts/…?expand=contracts`) was read first.
- Contract-level views used:
  - `getNumMomentsInEdition(setID, playID)`: minted count.
  - `getMomentsSubedition(nftID)`.
- Per moment: `getAccount(holder).capabilities.borrow<&{TopShot.MomentCollectionPublic}>(/public/MomentCollection).borrowMoment(id).data` gives `{setID, playID, serialNumber}`.
- Holder candidates came from three places: the latest `sales.buyer_address`, `topshot_ownership.owner_address` and `moments.owner_address`.
- Population: distinct `nft_id` on Top Shot base-edition `sales` with `serial_number > circulation_count`. That is 1,571 moments.
  - 357 have a candidate holder.
  - 241 were still held there and were read.

## Findings
- **`circulation_count` is right.** On-chain minted counts: 2:62 = 1000, 2:41 = 1000, 2:113 = 1530, 8:62 = 49, all equal to the catalog. #116 hypothesis 2 is refuted by the chain itself.
- **Of the 241 moments read:**
  - **214 (89 %) are on the WRONG edition with the RIGHT serial.** This is misattribution, the #82 shape.
  - **27 are on the right edition with a WRONG serial.**
  - **5 are parallels** (subedition 17).
  - Every chain edition exists in the catalog, and 240 of 241 chain serials fit that edition's circulation.
- **Example: $0.35–$3 sales of De'Andre Hunter "Clamps" (226:7541, COMMON) are filed under Giannis "Cosmic" (8:62, LEGENDARY /49).** 64 such rows exist.
  - They are older than 90 days (last 05-15), so the current 8:62 FMV ($2,550 LOW) does not read them.
  - The sales history of both editions is still wrong.
- **`moments` carries the same error.** For 136 of the 150 read moments that have a `moments` row, the row disagrees with the chain. Repointing them would cause 0 `(edition_id, serial_number)` collisions.
- **The inflow is closed.** The newest impossible-serial sale was ingested 2026-08-27, from any source, and none in the 14 days before this read.
