Source — v_topshot_pack_lifecycle_global / v_topshot_pack_lifecycle / v_topshot_pack_realized_ev. Minted / sealed / depletion are authoritative all-time supply from Top Shot's getPackListing.packListingContentRemaining, backfilled per dist (incl. sold-out) into pack_distributions and refreshed by cron. Opened / moments / realized $ come from the on-chain event tables and cover the ingest window (~Apr 10 2026 →) — so an old base-set dist can show high sealed/depletion but few in-window "opened" (it sold through before the window). Realized $ is FMV-based. Dist attribution of in-window opens ~21% (growing as the pool-attribution cron drains). Calibrated = a confidence-weighted blend of modeled EV toward realized (weight rises with sample size) — display-only, not the canonical pricing EV. Reload from the view header.
