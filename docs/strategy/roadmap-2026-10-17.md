# Rip Packs City — Roadmap, 2026-10-17

**Supersedes [roadmap-2026-08-03.md](roadmap-2026-08-03.md) as the canonical forward plan. It does not reverse it.** The 08-03 thesis stands word for word: **accuracy is the GATE, not a phase**. ## 1. The gate, unchanged — and what still binds

The four operational bars from 08-03 §1 (FMV reconcilable or `—`; pack EV against the remaining pool; sales history correct within a disclosed window; inventories complete or naming what they cannot price) are still the definition of "accurate". The guardrails that sit on top of them:

- **Read-only product.** No cart, trading, gifting (admin-signed giveaway delivery is the one exception). Never offer an action the product lacks. The 10-03 trading revisit ([trading-revisit-2026-10-03.md](trading-revisit-2026-10-03.md)) did not change this.
- **No paid tier shown or considered anywhere until 100 WAU** (raised from 50 on 09-25). **No infra spend pre-revenue.** **No outreach** (Trevor, 10-14).
- **A number we cannot stand behind renders as `—` with a reason.** A dead market gets a label, not a price (UFC).
- **Read a series, never a leg.** Every confidence share below is a weekly mean of the six-hourly `rpc_trust_health_history` legs; a single leg moves ±2.5 points on its own.
