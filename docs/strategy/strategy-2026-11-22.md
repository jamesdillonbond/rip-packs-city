### Drop week is where pricing was worst, and it is when collectors look hardest

The 11-11 Rookie Debut / Series 9 launch was the worst accuracy event on record for Top Shot, and nothing alarmed:

- **On 11-11, 37.6 % of Top Shot sales went through while the live price was ASK_ONLY.** Within-±25 % at sale fell from ~79 % to **47.8 %**. On 11-12 the ASK_ONLY share was 16.1 % and within-±25 % was 65.1 %.
- On Series 9 that week, **2,399 sales sold against an ASK_ONLY price at a median 2.16× the sale price**, within ±25 % only 8.8 %. That was measured at **lag 0**, the price live at the moment of sale, so it is not an artefact of the backtest's one-day lag.
- **Mechanism, read on one Rookie Debut edition:** one snapshot at 1:09 PM PT 11-11 ($0.33 ASK_ONLY), then **no new snapshot for ~20 hours while 37 sales cleared at ~$0.18**. The model was fine; repricing was too slow. When it did reprice, it read $0.18 MEDIUM.
- **The fix may already exist.** The fmv-recalc **hot lane** shipped 11-12 (`HOT_LANE_LIMIT 150`, `app/api/fmv-recalc/route.ts`). It reprices editions that sold since their last snapshot and lack sale-backed depth, which is exactly the drop-day population. Since 11-14, Series 9 ASK_ONLY at sale is down to 79 sales (ratio 1.64), and Top Shot overall reads ~80 % within ±25 % at sale. But the hot lane **has not faced a drop yet**: it handles ~7 editions per run today against a cap of 150. Rookie Debut alone is 152 editions.
- The weekly accuracy job will show it 11-23 (the 11-16 week). The 11-09 row already stores the failure as ASK_ONLY within-±25 % **18.4 %**, so the instrument saw it a week late, by design.

## 2. The strategy, in one paragraph

**Make the Top Shot portfolio-and-packs loop the product. Prove its pricing on the next drop. Run events on Top Shot's own calendar to pull collectors into that loop. And stop the estate growing: hold every other collection at "honest and maintained", no new surfaces.** Accuracy stays the gate, but measured the way a collector meets it: realised-sales accuracy on what trades, and on drop day.

## 3. Four moves

### Move 1 — Drop-day readiness (accuracy, Top Shot). *Claude Code can do this; no decision needed.*
1. ✅ **SHIPPED 11-22 ~9:45 AM PT** (`20261122173426`, sentinel arm "Top Shot Sales Against ASK_ONLY (6h)"; it warns at ≥ 10 % of ≥ 100 sales at ratio ≥ 1.5, not the lag form below). **A drop-day accuracy watch.** Add a sentinel / trust leg: the share of the last 6 h of Top Shot sales that sold against an ASK_ONLY price, plus the median sale → next-snapshot lag for editions under 7 days old. Warn at ASK_ONLY > 10 % of sales or lag > 2 h. On 11-11 this would have fired within hours; the weekly job saw it 12 days later.
2. **Make the hot lane drop-sized.** Today it reads ~7 editions a run. On a drop it must take every newly-minted edition that sold since its snapshot. Measure whether 150 per run at the current cadence clears a 152-edition drop inside 1 h; if not, give editions under 72 h old priority ahead of the cap.
3. **Rehearse on the next Top Shot drop.** Read the same query used here (lag-0 ASK_ONLY share and within-±25 % at sale, per PT day) on the drop day and the day after. Pass mark: within-±25 % at sale ≥ 70 % on drop day (11-11 read 47.8).

### Move 2 — Re-state the gate as realised-sales accuracy on what trades. *Needs Trevor.*
Proposal: the marketing gate for **Top Shot alone** becomes:
- HIGH and MEDIUM each ≥ 70 % within ±25 % of the sale price, for 4 consecutive weekly reads (the 11-02 and 11-09 weeks read 75.5 / 69.7 and 80.1 / 74.1);
- the drop-day pass mark from Move 1 on one real drop;
- M3–M11 unchanged.

All Day M2 stops gating Top Shot and stays a per-collection bar. The share-of-editions metric stays on the trust board as a coverage number, not a gate. This changes [go-live-2026-09.md](go-live-2026-09.md) §6, which is why it is Trevor's call. **What it unlocks:** the held items behind the gate, namely #66 external links and outreach, become open for Top Shot first.

### Move 3 — Events on Top Shot's calendar, feeding the loop. *Needs Trevor for dates and copy.*
- An event is the only thing that has moved signed-in users (36 in Dame Time week against ~20 without). Run one per Top Shot moment: a drop, a Rookie Debut tranche, a playoff set. Each event points at the loop: **paste your wallet → see your value → see your packs' EV.**
- Second-day return is the hole, not the wallet step: 31 of 34 signups since 11-02 saved a wallet. Email to members (weekly from 11-23, Day-1 live) is the second-day mechanism. Judge it on 11-24 by `utm_source=email` visits and returns, not by sends.
- Keep the guardrails: no paid tier before 100 WAU, no spend. Outreach waits on Move 2.

### Move 4 — Stop the estate growing. *Claude Code can do most of this; the cuts need Trevor.*
- **Lane budget:** from today, a new pg_cron job or pipeline ships only beside the retirement of one, recorded in the ledger. Target: 318 → under 280 active jobs by 12-31, cut by measuring yield the way the UFC lanes were cut on 10-18.
- **Maintenance mode** for Golazos ($38 / week), UFC (closed market), and the Pinnacle / Panini surfaces beyond what their members open: keep data honest, keep pages up, **no new features**. Panini's 26 jobs are the first place to measure yield per job.
- **Candy is the exception worth watching:** high-ticket and now trading. Keep its sales capture (the chain-sales lane with v1 support, buyer-paid pricing) healthy; no new surfaces until its members exceed 3.
- **Re-baseline Candy's trust row** on the per-mint grain (post-11-09) so the roadmap stops quoting 57.1 %.

## 5. What is not proposed

- No change to read-only, the trade room, the no-spend rule, or the 100 WAU paid-tier gate.
- No retirement of a collection's data or pages. Maintenance mode means no new work, not removal.
- No change to the honesty canon. The lane budget reduces the surface that canon has to cover; it does not loosen it.
